Clinical Data Science

CDC Validated Machine Learning & Calibration

Why raw AI predictions fail in healthcare, and how calibration fixes them.

Cohort & Model Specifics

  • CDC BRFSS Dataset: 253,680 adult patients, 21 clinical indicators.
  • Model: HistGradientBoosting with non-linear interaction learning.
  • External Validation: Independently tested on BRFSS 2021 cohort to ensure no temporal drift.
  • Preventive Threshold: 0.24 tuned for 80%+ recall to prevent dangerous false negatives.

Isotonic Probability Calibration

Raw machine learning classifiers suffer from uncalibrated probabilities. We train an Isotonic Regression mapping so that:

Predicted Risk 30% == 30 True Cases per 100 Patients

Ensures numbers shown to patients and clinicians reflect real statistical probability.

Screening Priority: Tuned to maximize sensitivity and catch at-risk patients early. Slide 6 of 10
Patient Transparency

Explainable AI: Counterfactual Risk Attribution

Moving beyond black-box scores into actionable, personalized drivers.

The Finite-Difference Engine

We compare the patient's risk against a calibrated neutral baseline:

ΔRisk = P(User Features) - P(User with Baseline Feature)

Identifies precisely how much each biomarker raises or reduces total risk.

Example Individualized Breakdown

  • High Blood Pressure: +11.4% risk increase
  • BMI of 31.2: +8.2% risk increase
  • Daily Vegetable Intake: -2.1% risk reduction
  • Active Exercise: -3.5% risk reduction (protective)

Shows patients what to change to see real risk reduction!

Global SHAP Insights: Top predictive factors are General Health, Blood Pressure, Age, BMI, and Cholesterol. Slide 7 of 10
